**HybridSliceCraft 0.9.0** brings painting beyond colour, import of your own presets, many more slicing options and more ways to send a print to your printer.

## Import your own presets

"Import profiles…" reads printer, filament and print settings that you exported from OrcaSlicer or Bambu Studio (plain files or their export bundles). Settings that have no counterpart here are listed in a report, so nothing is dropped silently. Only the files you choose are read.

## Paint more than colour

Next to filament colours, the paint tool can now mark parts of the surface:

- **Support enforcers**: supports appear where you paint, even when supports are off.
- **Support blockers**: no supports in the painted area, however far down it reaches.
- **Seam enforcers and blockers**: choose where the outer walls start and where they must not.
- **Fuzzy skin**: roughen only the painted part of the outer wall.

Marks are shown on the model and saved in the project.

## Seams, brim and supports

- **Scarf seam**: the outer wall fades in and out over a stretch at the seam, so it leaves no bump.
- **Mouse ears**: round brim pads only at sharp outer corners.
- **Grid style for supports**: a rectangle around each patch, sturdier and simpler to remove.

## Infill, surfaces and slicing

- Three new infill patterns: 2D lattice, 3D honeycomb and octagram spiral (17 in all).
- Top and bottom surface density, so surfaces can be filled less than solid.
- Thick bridges: round strands as wide as the nozzle, which sag less over long gaps.
- Hole size correction, closing small cracks in a model's skin, and polyholes, which make small round holes print to size.

## Height ranges and several filaments

- Give a model its own wall loops, infill density or infill pattern between two heights.
- A flush volume for each pair of filaments: the prime tower purges what each change needs.

## The bed and your printer

- No-go areas on the bed: they are drawn in red, avoided when arranging and flagged when slicing.
- Send a print to PrusaLink and Duet printers, as well as OctoPrint and Moonraker.

## Fixes

- Changing paint now marks the slice as out of date, so the preview can't show an old result.

## Good to know

- Prints that use none of the new settings produce the same G-code as before.
- Infill combination, variable-width walls and internal bridges are not part of this release.
- A separate filament for the prime tower is not available yet; the tower is printed by the tool that is loaded.

None of the saved files have been tried on a real printer yet. Check a small print first.
