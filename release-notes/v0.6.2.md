**HybridSliceCraft 0.6.2** makes slicing for filament printers faster again, most of all for models with many separate parts on each layer.

## Faster filament slicing

Compared with 0.6.1, measured on a 20-thread processor:

- A smooth wavy surface of 2.6 million triangles is sliced in about 7.5 s, down from about 48 s (and from over 10 minutes before 0.6.1).
- The Stanford dragon takes about 3.5 s, down from 4.1 s. With automatic supports it takes about 5.8 s, down from 7.7 s.

- **Skins and bridges** are worked out using only the part of the layer next to each island, instead of the whole layer. The G-code is exactly the same as before.
- **Automatic supports** are planned on several threads: supports that never touch each other, and separate pieces within one support, are worked out side by side.
- **Layer time estimate** is calculated once per layer unless the layer has to be slowed down. The G-code is the same.

## What changes in the G-code

Only automatic (grid) supports change, and only very slightly: on the dragon the estimated print time moves by about 0.04%. Tree supports and everything else stay the same.

## Not changed

The G-code writer still runs on one thread. None of the saved files have been tried on a real printer yet. Check a small print first.
