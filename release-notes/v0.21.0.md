**HybridSliceCraft 0.21.0** makes automatic supports better for both filament and resin, and gives resin rafts round vent holes that save resin.

## New

- **Resin supports hold the rims.** The sharp outer edges of flat undersides, where a layer starts to peel, now get supports all along, a little closer together than the rest. Where a ledge meets a wall going down, the wall holds it and no extra supports are added.
- **Filament tree supports follow "Only from the plate".** Branches steer around the model to reach the plate. A branch that can't is left out whole instead of standing on the model.

## Better

- **Raft vent holes** are round, sit on a staggered grid and are on by default: 2 mm holes every 3 mm take out about a quarter of a raft's resin. They still keep clear of the pillar feet and the raft's edge. Presets you saved with vent holes off keep them off.
- **Resin supports:**
  - Flat undersides keep the full density, while slopes that nearly hold themselves get fewer supports, so there are fewer marks to clean up.
  - Pillars taller than 20 mm get thicker, up to half as thick again at 50 mm, so they don't sway in the peel.
  - The raft keeps clear of any part of the model that stands on the plate.
  - Supports keep their distance from walls they run beside. A part that would otherwise fall off is still held, even through a tight gap.
- **Filament supports** that stand on the model leave the *Top gap* below them too, so they come off the surface they rest on instead of fusing to it.
- **For AI coding agents:** the source now has an `AGENTS.md` describing how to build, test and release the program.

Printer instructions change for models with supports: filament supports that rest on the model, tree supports with *Only from the plate*, and resin supports and rafts. Prints without supports are exactly the same as in 0.20.0.
