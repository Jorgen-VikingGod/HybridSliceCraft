**HybridSliceCraft 0.8.0** adds more control over filaments, layers and travel moves in filament (FDM) prints.

## A filament for the top and bottom surface

In a model's "This model only" panel you can now choose a filament for the top surface and the bottom surface on their own. Until set, they follow the solid infill filament.

## Pause, filament change or any G-code at a layer

In the preview, add a pause, a filament change or your own G-code at the layer the slider is on. They are saved in the project. A printer can set its own commands; otherwise the program uses the usual one for its firmware (for example M601 or PAUSE for a pause, M600 for a filament change).

## Machine limits in the G-code

Optionally send the printer's speed, acceleration, corner speed, and Z and extruder limits at the start of a print (Marlin, Klipper, RepRapFirmware). This is off by default.

## Acceleration, flow and fan for each kind of line

- Acceleration for inner walls, sparse infill, solid infill, top surface, bridges and the first layer
- Flow ratio for every kind of line, and for the first layer
- Fan speed for bridges and overhangs, support interface and ironing

## Z-hop

- Straight, slope or spiral lift
- Lift only between two heights
- Retract at every layer change

## Overhang speed steps

Walls slow down in four steps depending on how much of the line hangs in the air.

## Good to know

- Prints that use none of the new settings produce the same G-code as before.
- The prime tower is still printed by the tool that is loaded; it has no filament of its own.
- Skirt, brim and the prime tower still use the main tool's line width.
- Overhang fan speed does not yet depend on the overhang step, and curled perimeters are not slowed down separately.

None of the saved files have been tried on a real printer yet. Check a small print first.
