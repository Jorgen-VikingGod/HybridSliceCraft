**HybridSliceCraft 0.17.0** adds Z contouring for smoother curved and sloped tops.

## New

- **Z contouring** (also called Z anti-aliasing), in Print settings → Quality → Surface (shown with *Advanced*). Every layer leaves a small step on a slope or a curve. With Z contouring the nozzle moves up and down *within* a layer, so the top-surface lines, ironing and the walls just under the top follow the model's real surface. Domes, chamfers, rounded edges and gentle slopes come out smoother; the rest of the print keeps its layer height.
  - *Lowest contour height* (default 0.05 mm): the thinnest a contoured line may get.
  - *Lower walls steeper than* (default 35°): walls on steeper surfaces are lowered so their outer edge meets the slope.
  - *Keep fill direction*: solid and top lines run the same way on every layer.
  - A contoured line gets the plastic for its own thickness, and the preview shows it at the height it is printed.
  - Only surfaces that face up gain; the first layer and spiral vase stay flat. It is off by default, and with it off the printer instructions are exactly as before.
- Importing OrcaSlicer print profiles reads their Z contouring settings.
- For AI agents (MCP): the new settings are explained in `describe_settings`, and `slice_filament` reports how many lines were contoured.

None of the saved files have been tried on a real printer yet. Check a small print first.
