**HybridSliceCraft 0.21.1** puts the skirt round the whole print and adds full screen on F11.

## New

- **Full screen.** F11, or **View → Full screen**, fills the screen with the window and brings it back. You can give it another key in **Help → Keyboard shortcuts**.

## Better

- **The window opens where you left it** also when the interface is zoomed, and on Wayland it keeps its size from the last run.

## Fixed

- **The skirt goes round the whole print.** It used to follow only the first layer, so a part that leans out over a small base printed over its own skirt, and the preview showed the skirt running through the model. The skirt now keeps its distance from the widest part of the print, seen from above. Brim and raft still follow the first layer.

Printer instructions change only for the skirt of models that are wider higher up than at the bed: it moves out to clear them. Everything else is exactly the same as in 0.21.0.
