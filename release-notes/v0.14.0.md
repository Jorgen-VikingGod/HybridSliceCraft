**HybridSliceCraft 0.14.0** is about making the program fit the way you work. Most of these came from your issues. Thank you!

## Shorter lists

- **Printers by make.** Pick the make first, then the printer of that make.
- **Choose what is offered.** The new **Choose what is offered…** button opens a window where every printer, filament, resin and print quality can be ticked in or out. Hidden presets leave the drop-down lists but are not deleted; tick them again to bring them back. **Show everything** and **Only those in use** do it in one go. Your choice is saved.

## Working on the plate

- **Resize by percentage.** The percent value in the Placement card is now a field you can type in or drag. With several models selected they all change by the same percentage, handy for multi-part prints.
- **Select several models with a box.** Hold shift and drag over the view with the Move, Turn or Resize tool. Everything the box touches is added to the selection.

## Importing presets

- **Orca filament presets** exported by a user, which hold only what was changed, were taken for print-quality presets. That put the filament in the wrong list and gave odd layer settings. They are now recognised as filaments.
- **Resin presets.** Import profiles now also reads PrusaSlicer resin `.ini` files, a config bundle or a single exported preset, and the button is also in resin mode. You get the printer's display size and pixels, the resin's exposure times, density, colour and cost, and the print settings' layer height, fade layers, hollow wall, support sizes and pad.

## Fixes

- **Light Windows theme.** With Windows in light mode, pop-ups and some text came out light on the dark panels and were hard to read. The program now follows its own Dark or Light choice for everything, including the window's title bar.

All pictures in the README and the manual were made again.

## Good to know

- Filament printers are grouped by the first word of their name, so a few built-in generic ones sit under odd makes. Imported and your own printers will group as you name them.
- The PrusaSlicer import has been tried on made-up files that follow its format, not yet on a real bundle from the program. Tell us if a file doesn't read right.
- The Windows theme fix could not be tried on Windows before release.

None of the saved files have been tried on a real printer yet. Check a small print first.
