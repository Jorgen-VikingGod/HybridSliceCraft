**HybridSliceCraft 0.13.0** has a new look.

## Dialogs

- Every dialog has a fixed title bar with a close button and a fixed footer with its buttons and **Close**. Only the middle scrolls.
- When a dialog is taller than the window, a thin scroll bar stays in view. It gets wider when the pointer comes near.
- Soft shadows at the top and bottom of every scrolling area, including the side panels, show that there is more to see in that direction.

## A modern window frame on Linux

On Linux the program draws its own title bar and window edges instead of the system's plain frame: the title in the middle, round minimise, maximise and close buttons, rounded corners, and a thin outline. Drag the bar to move the window, double-click it to maximise, and drag the edges to resize.

If your desktop does it better, start the program with `HYBRIDSLICECRAFT_SYSTEM_FRAME=1` to keep the system's frame.

## Pictures

All pictures in the README and the manual were made again with the new look. `docs/make-pictures.sh` makes them all in one go.

## Good to know

- The new window frame has only been tried on one Linux setup so far. Tell us if the edges, corners or buttons misbehave on yours.
- The picture of tree supports in the manual still shows the old look.

None of the saved files have been tried on a real printer yet. Check a small print first.
