## New for filament printing

- **Paint tool:** pick a filament, then drag over the model to paint it. The brush size is adjustable.
- **Fill** colours a whole face or smooth patch at once and stops at sharp creases. **Erase** takes paint off. **Paint everything** and **Clear paint** work on the whole model.
- **Colour reaches in:** sets how far a painted colour goes into the part. Top and bottom surfaces colour the layers next to them too.
- Painted models print each colour with its own filament, using the prime tower for tool changes.
- Paint is saved in project files.
- Painting needs a printer with more than one filament slot, such as the dual extruder, four-colour changer or Snapmaker U1 presets.
- Not tried on a real printer yet.
