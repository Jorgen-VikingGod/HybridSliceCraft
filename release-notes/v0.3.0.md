**HybridSliceCraft 0.3.0** is a resin release: stronger supports and rafts, smarter orienting, a clearer report on floating parts, smoother layers, and a window you can make your own, with a light theme, three more languages and shortcuts you can change.

## New for resin printing

**Save for Elegoo printers**
- A new save format, GOO (version 3.0), next to SL1, PNG stack and CTB. Pick it in the format list when you save a print, or use `--export file.goo`.
- It hasn't been tried on a real printer yet. Start with a small test print.

**Supports and raft**
- **Light, Medium and Heavy** support presets, in the Supports card and in the print settings. They set how many supports are placed and how thick they are; the settings stay editable afterwards.
- **Raft vent holes** let resin flow through the raft and lower the pull while peeling. Choose the hole size and spacing; holes keep clear of the pillars and the raft's edge.
- **Raft edge slope** makes the foot of the raft wider than its top, so the edge doesn't lift off the plate.

**Orient for resin**
- Auto-orient now also avoids large flat roofs, which trap resin and pull hard when peeling, and it prefers a lower print and a wider footing over a tall, thin one.
- The Orient card shows how the model sits now: print height, area on the plate, flat roof area and area that needs support, with a hint when something looks risky.

**Floating parts**
- Every floating part is listed with its layer, height, position on the plate and area, biggest first.
- Parts are marked as large, small or speck, in the list and in the layer view, so tiny specks no longer look as serious as a real overhang.
- **Previous** and **Next** step through them in printing order. The warning names the biggest one.

**Z smoothing**
- Draws each layer at several heights and blends them, so sloped surfaces and the partly filled top of a model get smooth steps instead of hard ones.
- Three new settings in the Layers tab: Z smoothing (1 is off, up to 8), the smoothing curve and the dimmest grey.
- It is slower, about as many times as the number of samples, and is drawn on the processor.

## New for the workspace

**Mirror and copies**
- **Mirror** a model left-right, front-back or top-bottom, from the Edit menu, the right-click menu or the Placement card. Supports, drain holes and a hollow follow along.
- **Make several copies** (On the plate card) lays out a grid of copies with the space you choose, and warns if some reach past the plate.

**Light theme and more languages**
- **View → Theme** switches between dark and light. The 3D view and the layer picture stay dark in both.
- **View → Language** offers English, Deutsch, Français and Español. On first start the language of your system is used if it is one of these.
- Some tooltips and the status bar are still in English.

**Your own shortcuts**
- **Help → Keyboard shortcuts** is now a list you can edit. Click a key, press the new one. Clear or reset one command, or reset them all.
- Taking a key from another command tells you which one lost it. Menus and tooltips show the keys you chose.

## Upgrading

Projects and settings from 0.2.0 open as before. Theme and language are saved in `preferences.json`, and changed shortcuts in `hotkeys.json`, in the same settings folder as your printer presets. Z smoothing and raft vents are off by default, so existing prints slice the same way.

## Installers

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Windows | `.msi` installer |
| macOS, Apple silicon and Intel | `.dmg` disk image (one for each chip) |
| Linux | `.deb` package and a portable `.AppImage` |

The installers aren't code-signed yet. Windows SmartScreen and macOS Gatekeeper will warn on first launch; on macOS, right-click the app and choose Open.

## Known limits

- Files still haven't been tried on real printers. Start with a small print, especially with the CTB and GOO formats.
- The German, French and Spanish texts have not been reviewed by native speakers.
- Not included yet: more printer formats (CTB versions 2 to 5, NanoDLP), brush-painting colours onto a model, live printer camera and control, encrypted resin formats, and cloud model libraries.

The full feature list is in the [0.1.0 release notes](https://github.com/Jorgen-VikingGod/HybridSliceCraft/blob/v0.3.0/release-notes/v0.1.0.md).
